# Offer and scope — proposed, not yet market validated

## Offer

Finished product catalog and matching price sheet, produced from customer-approved source data for independent electrical and industrial distributors.

## Founding pilot

- Proposed price: **$750**.
- Maximum 50 unique sellable SKUs; one customer brand; one layout; one USD price tier.
- One source export; client-approved product descriptions and images.
- One proof and one consolidated revision round.
- Delivery date confirmed only after source inspection. Use five business days after complete intake as an initial scheduling hypothesis, then revise based on observed work.
- Confirm payment terms in the written scope and use an existing invoicing provider. The prototype does not accept payment.

## Target standard package after validation

- **$2,500 target** for up to 250 SKUs within the same input and revision limits.
- This is a proposed offer to test, not a verified average sale price.
- Larger catalogs, multiple tiers, complex variants, missing-data research, image editing, languages, or custom layouts require separate scope.
- Proposed updates: $250 per batch of up to 50 changed rows with the same source format and layout. Validate the effort before offering it.

## Deliverables

1. Digital catalog PDF and matching price-sheet PDF, created from visually inspected HTML.
2. Standalone searchable catalog HTML and matching price-sheet HTML.
3. Normalized formula-safe product CSV and exceptions CSV.
4. Review-record JSON, edition metadata, and a delivery note.
5. Customer keeps the original source and images; the operator keeps the approved production package in a private project location.

## Customer responsibilities

Provide accurate source product facts, current prices, correct selling units, and assets they may use. Identify the approver. Review and approve the proof and all unresolved warnings. A blank price is shown as “Price on request”; it is never inferred.

## Acceptance

Every supplied in-scope SKU appears exactly once in each output. Product counts, SKUs, prices, and selling units match the approved source, except documented normalization. Product/image matches are approved. No blocking issues remain. All PDF pages have been checked. Client provides written approval before final release.

## Change boundaries

One consolidated round of source corrections within the original product count and layout. New SKUs, additional price tiers, technical copywriting, and layout redesign are new scope. Replacing an inaccurate customer source is not silent permission to invent missing facts.

## Commercial reality

Revenue is not profit. Measure all operator and sales time, asset handling, revision costs, payment fees, taxes, and any subcontracting. No customer acquisition cost, conversion rate, margin, or willingness to pay has been established.

Do not sell ongoing maintenance or promise unattended automation until actual recurring update demand and safe approvals have been demonstrated.
