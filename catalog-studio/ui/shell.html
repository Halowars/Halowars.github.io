Before accepting a project

  1. Obtain a permitted 10-product source sample. Confirm one row per SKU, one USD price tier, a stated selling unit, and usable product photos.
  2. Agree on product count, layout, output formats, one revision round, and who approves product facts. Quote after inspecting the sample.
  3. Use an existing invoicing provider. Confirm scope and payment before production. There is no payment checkout in this prototype.

Production and review

  1. Keep the original source unchanged in the customer's private project folder. Never commit customer files to this source repository.
  2. Import the CSV, review mapping, load approved photos and logo, and resolve every blocking issue with the customer. Do not guess specifications or prices.
  3. Export the exceptions report and cleaned CSV. Reconcile product count and each SKU, selling unit, and price with the source.
  4. Export both draft HTML files. Open each locally, search for a SKU, and inspect every category. Print to A4 PDF with browser headers and footers off and background graphics on.
  5. Inspect all PDF pages for clipped names, split cards, missing images, incorrect prices, and blank extra pages. Browser print settings vary; this step is mandatory.
  6. Send the draft and collect written approval of content, images, prices, selling units, and acceptable warnings. Keep that approval outside the tool.
  7. Acknowledge the release checkbox, regenerate both final files, save the final review record, and inspect the final PDFs again.

Delivery package

  • Final digital catalog PDF and matching price sheet PDF, generated from the inspected HTML.
  • Self-contained catalog HTML and price sheet HTML for offline search and future PDF export.
  • Cleaned product CSV, exceptions CSV, and review record JSON. Import the SKU column into spreadsheet software as text to preserve leading zeros.
  • A short delivery note with edition date, approved unresolved warnings, and the update policy.

Version 0.1 is an operator prototype. Real browser layout, printing, image import, and customer-data trials have not yet passed acceptance. Do not represent it as fully tested or sell an unattended service.

Exactly what Tristan does next

  1. Complete the browser acceptance checks with the fictional sample and deliberately broken sample.
  2. Pick 10 suitable distributors. Prefer a clear evidence point: an outdated PDF, a confusing price sheet, or a supplier line without usable sales materials.
  3. Prepare a 10-product proof only from public information you may use or a sample they explicitly authorize. Do not invent product facts or use unauthorized photos.
  4. Send a small number of individual messages after checking applicable outreach requirements. Keep prospecting and sending manual for the pilot.
  5. Show one source row beside the resulting catalog entry on a short call. Sell the finished package, then ask for a paid pilot.
  6. Measure total production time and revision time. If 50 products take more than four operator hours, narrow scope or raise the price before repeating.
