## Demonstrate on the buyer's approved sample

1. Reconcile SKU counts and leading-zero identifiers against the source. Confirm the mapping of list price, cost and selling unit explicitly.
2. Independently calculate at least five line prices, including a half-cent rounding case, and check every tier. Independently calculate a margin at and below the selected floor.
3. Introduce one duplicate SKU, missing cost and below-floor dealer price in a test copy. Each must block customer export.
4. Compare previous/current snapshots containing an added SKU, removed SKU, changed cost, changed price and changed selling unit. Confirm changes are visible and release needs renewed review.
5. Export every tier to HTML and CSV. Verify costs, margins, source rows and other tiers do not appear in customer files. Keep internal exports separate.
6. Print the actual sample and one longest-text example using the buyer's intended browser/settings. Inspect every page for clipping, legibility, correct prices/units and useful page breaks.
7. Load supplied images and logo. Save the private project, close the program, reopen the project, confirm data/photos and verify that approval is cleared.
8. Operate once with networking disabled. Confirm no seller account, secret, server or runtime installation is required.
9. Have the buyer perform one complete cycle using only `BUYER_GUIDE.md`. Resolve confusing instructions before acceptance; do not declare the product self-service solely from a developer demo.

## Close the project

Record outstanding reproducible in-scope defects, correct them, rerun affected checks and obtain acceptance against the agreed version. Process reports submitted within the fixed defect window. Once those obligations are complete, mark the delivery closed. Further development requires a separate scope; the buyer can also use its own developer because source/build instructions are supplied.

Future browser changes and untested environments cannot be covered by a claim of perfect compatibility. The acceptance record names what was actually tested and accepted.
