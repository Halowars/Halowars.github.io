02

Set the commercial rules.

Dealer prices are discounts from your supplied list price. Gross margin = (selling price − cost) ÷ selling price. Values below your minimum block customer exports.

Prices are never changed to meet a margin target automatically. Correct your source or approved discounts.

QUICK GUIDE

A repeatable publishing cycle.

  1. Open last project's backup, or import a full product CSV and match its columns. Use only approved data.
  2. Check your rules. Costs and selling prices must use the same currency and selling unit. Clear each blocking issue.
  3. Inspect every tier's proof. Missing images are explicit. Verify the HTML and printed PDF against your source.
  4. Release and save. Export customer books separately. Save a private project backup, source files and review record before closing.

No website integration or server is needed. This version does not synchronize computers, connect to an ERP, change inventory, calculate taxes, convert selling units, or send customer messages. Share exported customer files through your existing channels.

Closing the tab clears the workspace. Backups are ordinary files on your device and contain costs; use your business's normal device security and backups.
