# Market evidence and limits

Reviewed October 20–21, 2026. Prices are vendor-published, may change and are not directly equivalent to this product. No competitor price proves a buyer will pay our proposed price.

1. 65bit official pricing: https://www.65bit.com/pricing/
   EasyCatalog $1,299 per user; Pagination Module $599; EasyCatalog Server $7,500 for the first volume tier, before local taxes. This is evidence that a basic catalog-generation feature alone has cheaper established alternatives. The seller's offer needs a more specific business outcome and credible delivery.

2. Pagination official pricing: https://pagination.com/pricing/
   The retrieved page lists Professional €100/month and Business €360/month per template, billed annually, with enterprise pricing by quote. It illustrates that recurring catalog systems exist; a local perpetual handover is a different commercial model, not an automatic quality advantage.

3. Catalog Automation official service illustration: https://www.catalogautomation.com/
   The vendor illustrates a $15,000 automated solution composed of $10,000 professional services, $3,000 software and $2,000 internal labor, versus $26,000 manual production. These are that vendor's hypothetical scenario, not verified customer outcomes or evidence that our reusable program can command $15,000 without services.

4. Playwright official CI documentation: https://playwright.dev/docs/ci
   Reference for browser installation and reproducible CI execution. Tests are implementation evidence, not proof of product-market fit.

The $12,500 proposed license, time/value qualification rule, target segment, fixed acceptance process and handover model are our commercial hypotheses. Validate them with buyer data and purchasing decisions. No sales or performance promises are inferred from vendor marketing.
