# Sources and commercial assumptions

Researched October 20, 2026.

## Primary competitor evidence

- Catalog Machine product overview: https://www.catalogmachine.com/
  The vendor advertises PDF/online catalog creation, templates, CSV product import, and automated data organization.
- Catalog Machine pricing/FAQ: https://www.catalogmachine.com/pricing/
  The vendor describes AI-assisted spreadsheet-to-catalog workflows, no setup fee, and subscription plans. Some exact plan prices were dynamically rendered in the retrieved page, so this kit does not assert those values.

## What that evidence means

Generic “upload CSV and make a catalog” is already available. A $2,500 package cannot be justified merely by having coded a generator. The proposed service instead bundles source-data reconciliation, careful photo matching, complete document production, proof handling, and delivery.

That positioning is a business inference, not proven demand. The $750 pilot, $2,500 standard target, $250 update target, five-business-day scheduling hypothesis, and four-hour operator threshold are proposed experiments. None are market averages, demonstrated margins, or promised revenue.

No customer interviews, sales, paid trials, or conversion data have been collected for this product.
